Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Summary

Little Haven does not collect any personal data. There is no account, no advertising, no analytics or tracking, and no server of our own. The app works completely offline.

Data in the app

Stored on your device: your saved game (village, villagers, progress) and your settings (e.g. language, sound, notifications). This data stays on your device and is never transmitted.

Notifications: if you allow them, the game reminds you with local notifications, for example when a building is finished. They are created on the device; no server and no push service is involved.

Time and date: used locally to show village days, time of day and seasons.

In-app purchase

The “Unlock the Full Game” purchase is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We receive no payment or contact details. Apple’s privacy policy applies: apple.com/legal/privacy.

Crash reports

If you choose to share analytics with app developers in iOS Settings, Apple provides us with anonymized crash reports. You control this in your system settings.

This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub Inc., USA). GitHub processes technically necessary data such as your IP address to deliver the page and prevent abuse. See the GitHub Privacy Statement. We use no cookies and no analytics.

Email

If you write to us, we use your details only to answer your request and delete them once they are no longer needed.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability and objection, and you may lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

Children

Little Haven is suitable for all ages and collects no data from children either.
